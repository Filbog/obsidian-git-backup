Electronic 1st curtain is like a hybrid between mechanical and electronic shutter. In most of the scenarios, best to use this one. 
We want to use **electronic** if:
 - we need super fast shutter speeds
 - we need maximum FPS burst on our cameras
 - we want a completely silent camera
However, using electronic has downsides:
 - potentially reducing the dynamic range
 - you can't shoot at very slow shutter speeds like 1s
