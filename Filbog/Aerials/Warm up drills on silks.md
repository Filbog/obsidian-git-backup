## knot
 - cobra front balance
 - Front balance pike to cobra
 - Back balance - plank/hollow body. Legs frog, straddle, together
 - Side balance - same as above
 - C-shaping; top-bottom heavy, rocks etc
 - Waist rolls
## knot super low
 - plank tucks, legs in knot
 - Plank press to HS drill, legs in knot
 - Same as above, but one leg is free and goes to straight hip. Can be dangerous if you can’t handstand and/or bail out of handstand!

## low on the ground 
 - scap work - hands in front, back. Easier legs bent, harder legs straight
 - Australians
 - Climbing hands only, legs on floor
