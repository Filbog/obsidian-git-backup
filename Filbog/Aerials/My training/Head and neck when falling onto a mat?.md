### from trampoline:
Trampolinists teach that your whole upper body should be flat during back drops, including the head. So that the impact is distributed evenly. So head tucked in during landing on ur back - NO. But it’s not completely relaxed either, to avoid it banging against the tramp/mat and whiplash.
