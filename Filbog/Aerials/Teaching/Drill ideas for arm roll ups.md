You start either with an extra wrap, or normally. 
If normally - u start to roll up
If extra wrap - you start to roll down.
Then, when upside down, you want to tag the feet (like upside down cobra)to take some weight off of the shoulders. And there, in the upside down position, you want to go up/down to feel the tension needed to hold yourself in the correct position
