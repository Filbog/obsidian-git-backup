## interesting stuff from lessons
### 1arm flare to flag
- look at the feet - helps to stay hollow and orient yourself in the air
### 1arm flare to nutcracker
#### common mistakes
- not looking at the feet
- pike to early
### bottom switch
- when opening to flag, we can use our arm to twist by throwing the arm to the back
### 2arm disloc
- push onto the straps
### 1arm disloc
- arm ER on top height
### 1arm inloc
- look at the floor as long as possible
- arm IR
### 1arm soleils
- arm IR in the first half
- he says first half of the turn starts after the first quarter, halfway in the front swing
#### common mistakes
- going into disloc and twisting after
### mushu
- when in full position, the top leg has to go down first; without it, the twist is harder
### 1arm press to flag/low straddle switch
- look at your feet until you're high enough, helps with compression and knowing where you are
### 1arm swing to flag
- twist late, almost wait to until you're inverted
- twist with the same-side leg/hip
- look at your feet
### 1arm full dropout
- head stays down, legs up during the untwist
- look at the floor
- open your legs when you're passing the straps, so that you're ready to fall into the front swing
### 1arm spinning flag dropout
- to initiate: straighten the hanging arm and dive with the upper body
- you want to think as if you switch legs in flag: bottom leg goes up, top leg goes down (hip flexion)
	- the top leg kinda kicks around, look vid above
### 1arm spinning full dropout
- easier in tuck
- upper body dives down first
### 1arm full to butt-through
- grab high, he says as high as possible
- hip compression importante
- using the momentum to kind of rebound at the bottom
- (kinda looks like reverse spin meathook to flag swing)
### 1arm full to open grip back balance
- same principles as 1arm full to butt-through
### 2arm swing to sit or deltchev
- similar to back salto, but arms wide and legs even wider
### 1arm inside pirouette
- free arm starts working after we kick and swing
- legs higher than 45deg
- look at the ground during the pirouette part
### 1arm back swing to reverse full/mushu
- basically applies to all the swing-to-waist-roll stuff: we have to be way more inverted than when doing the same skills from spinning
### 1arm back swing to sit arm-in-back
- hanging arm IR
- in the vid he goes a bit thru the side, the hanging arm is more on the same-side-leg side
- try to sit on the strap, not on your wrist
### 1arm back swing to sit arm-in-front
- I think you can do this from either back swing or front swing?
- kinda like 2arm swing to sit/deltchev
### 1arm outside pirouette
- push the straps away, like face pull-ish
- it has to be **horizontal**!
- spot the floor at the end of the twist
### rvr spin flare/kick
- legs kick down and crossed
- arm IR
### 1arm flag tuck dropout
- look down while droppiung from flag, then over your hanging arm to see your legs
### 2arm spinning swan front lever
- pull wide, arms away from your chest
### front balance split
- spot on the front foot
### kip to armpit swing
- pull - arms IR
