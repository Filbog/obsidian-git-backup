### **aerial performances** 
 - AT LEAST 1/250 for static stuff. 1/400 preferable
 - 1/400 for some transitions and "slower" drops
 - **1/800** **is optimal for 90% of performances, including drops and some dynamic stuff**
 - ??? for crazy twists and very fast drops

### **aerial photoshoots** 
 - AT LEAST 1/160 if we have another person to totally stop the movement of the subject
 - 1/250 is relatively safe, 1/400 is optimal for static stuff
 - dynamic stuff, drops, waving the silk - if we want to freeze the motion, look up aerial performances above

### floor acrobatics
 - **AT LEAST 1/400** - floor acro is all about movement, so we want to freeze the motion
 - 1/800 is OK if we want to freeze the motion in "slower", more controlled elements like front walkover, cartwheel
 - ??? for elements like front/backflips, flash kicks
